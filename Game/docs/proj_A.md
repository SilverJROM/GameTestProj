# Project A — Multiplayer game

Build a small multiplayer game with persistent state.

## Project requirements

You choose the rules, scope, implementation and intended player experience.

The applicant must choose one route:

- **Persistent economy:** An ongoing shared world with resources, progression, and consequential interaction between players, inspired by games such as Legend of the Red Dragon or TradeWars.
- **Compact 4X:** A bounded campaign with exploration, expansion, resource exploitation, and combat, including a map, resources, units, and multiple victory conditions. Diplomacy and technology trees are optional.

The applicant must deliver and substantiate the following:

- Players can interact and progress through the chosen route, with both player-versus-player (PvP) and player-versus-environment (PvE) play.
- State changes follow the published rules, preserve consistent resources and ownership, and persist correctly.
- The application includes functioning authentication and authorization, with mechanisms and an access model chosen by the applicant.
- **The applicant must take great care to establish functional balance in PvP and PvE, including how they affect each other.** Their work must define and substantiate meaningful choices, counterplay, workable progression, and recovery under realistic play. Numerical symmetry or equal win rates alone do not establish balance.
- Reproducible simulations exercise the implemented rules across differing strategies, starting conditions, and sustained play. Demonstrations through the hosted game support the applicant's claims; failures and limits must be reported honestly.

Keep game rules, simulations and supporting evidence in your submission repository.

See the Developer Brief in the portal for the shared delivery requirements, timing and submission steps.
